Privacy

Storybook is a class project (MCON 368). Sign in with Google gives it your name, email address and profile picture.

The photo you upload and your book's pictures are stored in the app's database and shown only to your account. To make the pictures, the photo is sent to OpenAI's API. Only upload a photo you have the right to use, and for a child, only with a parent's permission.

Payments run on Stripe's sandbox. No real money moves, and the app never sees card details.

You can delete any book from your shelf, which removes its photo and pictures. To have your whole account deleted, contact the course's student through the class.
